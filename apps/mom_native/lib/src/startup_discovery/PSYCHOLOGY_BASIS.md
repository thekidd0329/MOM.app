# Startup discovery psychology basis

This system is not a diagnostic test and does not assign a clinical personality label. It creates a revisable working user model from the user's own answers so MOM can adapt how she remembers, prompts, communicates, supports decisions, and asks follow-up questions.

## Primary foundations

### Big Five / Five-Factor personality research
The broad personality signals use the well-established dimensions of openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, and emotional reactivity/neuroticism as descriptive anchors rather than fixed types.

Reference:
- APA Dictionary of Psychology, Big Five personality model: https://dictionary.apa.org/big-five-personality-model
- APA Dictionary of Psychology, five-factor personality model: https://dictionary.apa.org/five-factor-personality-model

### Self-Determination Theory
Motivation and support routing includes autonomy, competence, and relatedness. These matter directly to how MOM should offer choices, challenge the user, provide structure, and decide whether connection or independent action is more useful.

Reference:
- Self-Determination Theory overview: https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/the-theory/

### Motivational Interviewing communication principles
The discovery conversation favors open exploration, autonomy support, affirmation, reflection, and avoiding an expert/interrogation posture. MOM should draw information out rather than behave like a test proctor.

Reference:
- Motivational Interviewing Network of Trainers, Understanding Motivational Interviewing: https://motivationalinterviewing.org/understanding-motivational-interviewing

### Attachment-related support patterns
Relationship questions track support preferences such as reaching toward others for reassurance versus pulling inward for space. They are stored as practical support preferences, not attachment diagnoses.

Reference:
- APA Dictionary of Psychology, Adult Attachment Interview: https://dictionary.apa.org/adult-attachment-interview

## Design rules

1. No answer permanently defines the user.
2. Mixed or context-dependent answers lower certainty and invite more discovery.
3. Clear answers can shorten setup.
4. Sensitive branches appear only when earlier answers make them relevant.
5. The startup path has a hard ceiling of 20 questions even though the bank contains 200 discoveries.
6. Unasked discoveries remain available for future natural conversation.
7. MOM should confirm consequential inferences before treating them as facts.
8. The profile is a working map for personalization, not a diagnosis or psychological score report.
