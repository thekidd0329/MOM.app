# PROPOSED: Initial Assessment Structure

Status: proposed.

The assessment is a concentrated first learning session, potentially around an hour, but it should feel like a conversation rather than a form.

Proposed areas:
- communication style;
- routines and schedule;
- goals and unfinished commitments;
- common forgotten things;
- important people and relationships;
- important places and objects;
- reminder preferences;
- interruption tolerance;
- humor and profanity boundaries;
- what "help" usually makes things worse;
- privacy and permission expectations;
- topics where MOM should ask before assuming;
- what the user wants MOM to notice over time.

Requirements:
- safe pause/resume;
- confirmed answers persist;
- no need to repeat already-confirmed information;
- the user can correct anything later;
- unanswered sections remain unknown rather than guessed.
