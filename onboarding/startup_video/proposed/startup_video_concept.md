# PROPOSED: Startup Video Concept

Status: proposed.

A guy wakes up late and already behind. He scrambles to get dressed, wakes the baby, shaves and brushes his teeth in a rush, gets a shirt on wrong, drops things, and leaves the room more chaotic than he found it.

His girlfriend is exhausted by the pattern. At one point she texts "mom":
> i dont know how much more of this man i can handle

He reaches the car, opens a glovebox full of clutter, realizes something is missing, runs back inside and asks:
> Keys?

She gives him the look, then:
> Uhh, kiss??

He gives her a rushed half-assed kiss and bolts.

The video later reveals several small details the viewer was expected to notice, widening the point beyond this one chaotic morning. One future-life beat can include a kid graduating and texting a friend:
> i did it bro!

End idea: everybody needs a mom, not because everybody needs to be babied, but because life leaks details everywhere and somebody has to remember the shit that matters.
